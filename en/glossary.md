# Glossary

## HumanLifeOS {#humanlifeos}

HumanLifeOS (HLOS) is a scientific, engineered, lifestyle-integrated, and systematic human life intelligence operating system.

## HLOS {#hlos}

Abbreviation for HumanLifeOS.

## RA {#right-awakening}

Right Awakening — an upgrade of traditional mindfulness, expanding from simple "awareness" to "awareness + understanding + action."

## RAT-1 {#right-awakening-training}

Right Awakening Training Level 1 — foundation training for developing stable awareness.

## RAT-2 {#right-awakening-therapy}

Right Awakening Training Level 2 — therapeutic applications for anxiety, depression, and stress.

## RAC {#right-awakening-camp}

Right Awakening Camp — intensive residential training program.

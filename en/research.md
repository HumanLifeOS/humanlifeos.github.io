# Research

Explore cutting-edge research on mindfulness and neuroscience.

## Goals {#goals}

### Core Objectives

- Validate the effectiveness of Right Awakening training through scientific methods
- Explore the neural mechanisms of consciousness and awareness
- Develop engineering-grade life training systems
- Establish standardized assessment metrics for life intelligence

### Long-term Vision

Build a scientific foundation for life intelligence engineering, making life training as systematic, measurable, and replicable as software engineering.

## Projects {#projects}

### Active Projects

#### Right Awakening Effectiveness Study
A longitudinal study measuring the effects of RA training on attention, emotion regulation, and well-being.

#### Neural Correlates of Awareness
fMRI-based research investigating brain changes associated with long-term awareness practice.

#### AI-Assisted Life Training
Developing AI systems to provide personalized life training guidance based on individual patterns.

### Collaborative Projects

Partnerships with universities and research institutions on life sciences, psychology, and AI research.

## Research Directions

### Neuroscience Research

- Effects of mindfulness on brain structure
- Neural plasticity from long-term practice
- Neural mechanisms of mindfulness and emotion regulation

### Psychology Research

- Effects of mindfulness on mental health
- Relationship between mindfulness and well-being
- Applications of mindfulness in clinical treatment

# HLOS Open Ecosystem & Licensing

## 1. Open Philosophy

HumanLifeOS (HLOS) aims to build a human intelligence infrastructure for the future.

We believe the evolution of human knowledge, intelligence training methodologies, and technological tools requires global collaboration and continuous innovation.

Therefore, HumanLifeOS adopts a **Progressive Open Ecosystem Model**:

* Software will be progressively open sourced;
* Knowledge will be continuously shared;
* Methodologies will be open for research;
* Communities will co-create the ecosystem.

Open does not simply mean unrestricted copying. It means building a sustainable ecosystem based on respect, contribution, quality, and shared development.


## 2. Layered Open Model

HumanLifeOS applies different licensing approaches to different types of assets.

### 2.1 Software & Technology

Includes:

* Source code;
* Developer tools;
* AI Agent frameworks;
* Data processing tools;
* Infrastructure components.

License:

**Apache License 2.0 / MIT License**

Permitted:

* Use;
* Modification;
* Distribution;
* Commercial application.

The specific license is defined in each repository's LICENSE file.


### 2.2 Knowledge & Content

Includes:

* Life Manual;
* HumanLifeOS Knowledge Base;
* Public educational materials;
* Research documentation.

License:

**Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)**

Allowed:

* Learning;
* Sharing;
* Modification;
* Derivative creation.

Requirements:

* Attribution required;
* Derivative works must remain under the same license.


### 2.3 Research & Scientific Materials

Includes:

* Experimental data;
* Research reports;
* Scientific validation materials.

License:

**Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**

Allowed:

* Citation;
* Analysis;
* Research;
* Reuse.

Requirements:

* Proper attribution;
* Scientific integrity maintained.


### 2.4 Methodologies & Frameworks

Includes:

* HumanLifeOS conceptual framework;
* Four-dimensional methodology:

  * Scientificization;
  * Engineering;
  * Lifestyle integration;
  * Systemization;
* RA (Right Awakening) training framework.

HumanLifeOS encourages:

* Academic research;
* Non-commercial education;
* Community discussion.

Commercial applications, certified training programs, and derivative services should follow HumanLifeOS authorization policies.


## 3. Trademark & Brand Protection

The following are protected HumanLifeOS ecosystem identities:

* HumanLifeOS™
* HLOS™
* RA™ (Right Awakening)
* Right Awakening Training™
* Life Manual™

Open content does not grant permission to use HumanLifeOS branding.

Unauthorized use includes:

* Impersonating official HumanLifeOS identity;
* Creating misleading affiliation;
* Commercial use without authorization.

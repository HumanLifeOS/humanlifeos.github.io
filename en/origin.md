# Origin

## Life OS {#lifeos}

Humans are born with a "factory reset" but never receive a manual for operating their own life. Computers come pre-installed with operating systems, phones come pre-installed with firmware, yet humans — the most complex intelligent life form on Earth — are born without any built-in instructions.

### 1. Humans Need a Life Operating System

Computers have operating systems (Windows, macOS); AI agents have operating systems (Agentic OS) — they manage hardware resources, schedule computing tasks, and provide interaction interfaces. Humans need just such a system: managing attention resources, scheduling cognitive tasks, and providing an interface between self and world.

### 2. The Inevitable Path of Human Evolution

Human evolution necessarily progresses from Instinct OS to Intelligent OS.

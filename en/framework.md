# Framework

## Preface

**Traditional life exploration needs to evolve from experience to method, from individual wisdom to engineering-based replicable systems.**

Human exploration of life, consciousness, and happiness has long faced three core issues:
1. **Lack of scientific validation**: Life wisdom relies heavily on personal experience, making it difficult to establish unified standards
2. **Lack of engineering paths**: We know the direction but lack systematic training processes from cognitive change to behavioral change to life state change
3. **Lack of life integration**: Traditional practices are often divorced from modern life, unable to adapt to ordinary people's work, family, and social environments

HumanLifeOS re-examines the life system from first principles:
Human life is first driven by the **Instinct OS**, forming automated behavior patterns through survival, security, control, recognition, and care instincts. But humans possess higher-level intelligent capabilities. Through awareness, understanding, and training, we can achieve a system upgrade from: "Instinct-driven → Intelligent awareness → Intelligent autonomy → Life evolution".

## Scientific Methodology {#scientific-methodology}

Applying first principles thinking, empirical validation, and systematic observation to the study of life.

## Systematization & Engineering {#systemization-engineering}

Transforming life wisdom into engineerable, replicable systems through structured methodologies and processes.

## Lifestyle Training {#lifestyle-training}

Integrating awareness practice into daily life — work, relationships, and social environments.

## Research Directions {#scientific-research}

Exploring the frontiers of life intelligence through interdisciplinary collaboration across neuroscience, psychology, AI, and philosophy.

## Training Framework

### Awakening Training System

Awakening Training is the core methodology of HumanLifeOS, divided into four levels:

1. **Foundation**: Breath awareness and body scan
2. **Perception**: Emotion recognition and cognitive insight
3. **Thinking**: Mental patterns and belief deconstruction
4. **Wisdom**: Transcending ego and unity experience

## Technical Means

### Modern Tools

- **Neural Feedback**: Monitoring physiological indicators through wearables
- **AI Assistance**: Intelligently analyzing training data, providing personalized suggestions
- **Community Support**: Online community interaction, growing together

## Practice Path

### Progressive Approach

1. Daily practice (10-30 minutes)
2. Deep training (weekend workshops)
3. Long-term practice (annual training camps)

# Training

## What is Right Awakening? {#right-awakening}

Right Awakening (RA) is an upgrade of traditional mindfulness — expanding from simple "awareness" to a complete system of "awareness + understanding + action."

## Mindfulness Training {#right-awakening-training}

Foundation-level training for developing stable awareness.

### Course Content
- Breath awareness
- Body scan meditation
- Emotion recognition
- Present-moment awareness

### Format
- Online courses: 7-day introduction
- Daily practice: 15-30 minutes

## Mindfulness Therapy {#right-awakening-therapy}

Therapeutic applications for emotional healing and mental health.

### Applications
- Anxiety reduction
- Depression management
- Stress relief
- Emotional regulation

## Training Camp {#right-awakening-camp}

Intensive residential programs for deep immersion.

### Schedule
- Weekend camps: 2-3 days
- Monthly retreats: 7 days
- Annual intensive: 30 days

## Course System

### Beginner Courses
- **Mindfulness Basics**: 7-day introduction course
- **Breath Awareness**: Focused breath practice
- **Body Scan**: Full body relaxation training

### Advanced Courses
- **Emotional Management**: Emotion recognition and regulation
- **Thinking Training**: Cognitive restructuring techniques
- **Stress Management**: Stress reduction strategies

### Professional Courses
- **Deep Meditation**: Long-term meditation guidance
- **Mindful Leadership**: Leadership mindfulness training
- **Mindfulness Coaching**: Professional coaching certification

## Training Formats

### Online Courses
- Video instruction
- Live interaction
- Community discussion

### Offline Workshops
- Intensive training
- Instructor guidance
- Peer support

## Training Plan

### Weekly Schedule
- Monday-Friday: Daily practice (15 minutes/day)
- Weekends: Deep training (2 hours)

# HLOS Training Camp
> —— HLOS Training Camp is a training system based on Right Awakening (RA), consisting of online communities and offline clubs. Right Awakening is the direct recognition and retraining of the operating mechanisms of life.

## I. What is Right Awakening Training? {#right-awakening-training}
> —— From understanding life to upgrading life

### 1. The Self-Limitation of Life
HLOS holds that: the greatest limitation of human beings is not insufficient knowledge, but that the life system has long operated in **the instinct-driven mode without awareness and introspection (Instinct OS)**.
### 2. The Patterns of Life
Emotions, desires, habits, fears, and cognitive patterns continuously influence our judgments and behaviors.
### 3. Life Needs an Upgrade
We possess a highly developed brain, yet we are often driven by unexamined internal programs. Therefore, humanity needs a system upgrade: from an unaware instinct-driven system to an aware intelligence-driven system, naturally giving rise to a state of wisdom.

> Right Awakening Training (RAT) is the life intelligence upgrade training system proposed by HLOS.

## I. Two Cores of Right Awakening Training {#right-awakening-core}

### 1. Recognizing the Essence of Awareness
From "I have consciousness" to "recognizing consciousness itself." Through systematic insight training, explore:
- What is awareness?
- What is the self?
- How does thought arise?
- How do emotions form?
- How do habits control behavior?
> This stage is similar to the foundational model training of AI, establishing a basic understanding of one's own life system.


### 2. Training the Intelligence Control System
From automatic reaction to active choice, train:
- Emotional awareness
- Mind-body connection
- Thought observation
- Behavioral adjustment
- Inner stability

> Ultimately form: Awareness → Understanding → Choice → Action → Feedback → Evolution — a closed loop of life intelligence.


## II. Architecture of Right Awakening Training System {#right-awakening-architecture}
> —— The architecture of the Right Awakening Training system includes four levels: Cognitive Level, Awareness Level, Reconfiguration Level, and Intelligence Level. Each level has specific goals and training content.

### 1. Cognitive Level Understanding

Recognize the life operating system, learn:
* Structure of the human life system
* Instinct-driven mechanisms
* Laws of consciousness operation
* Relationship between emotions and behavior

> Goal: Establish a foundational model of the Life OS.


### 2. Awareness Level Awareness

Train the ability to observe oneself, including:
* Body awareness
* Emotional awareness
* Thought awareness
* Self awareness

> Goal: Gain real-time monitoring capability of the life system.


### 3. Reconfiguration Level Reconfiguration

Optimize internal operating programs, including:
* Belief upgrade
* Emotion management
* Habit reshaping
* Behavior optimization

> Goal: Migrate from the old system to the new system.


### 4. Intelligence Level Intelligence

Let intelligence naturally emerge, manifested as:
* Clearer cognition
* More stable emotions
* Freer choices
* More harmonious relationships
* Higher-level life state

> Goal: Become a "New Human."



## Why Does Life Need Training? {#right-awakening-training-reason}
> —— Knowledge does not automatically change life

Traditional education mainly trains:

* Information acquisition ability
* Logical analysis ability
* Professional skills ability

But rarely trains:

* Ability to be aware of oneself
* Ability to manage emotions
* Ability to know oneself
* Ability to transcend automatic reactions

Therefore:

> Humans possess increasingly powerful external tools, yet are still often limited by internal systems.

In the AI era, humans not only need external intelligence tools, but even more need to upgrade their own life intelligence.

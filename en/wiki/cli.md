# Life Healing

Approaches to healing and restoring balance in the life system.

## Types of Life Imbalance

- **Emotional imbalance**: Anxiety, depression, anger, grief
- **Cognitive imbalance**: Rigid thinking, cognitive distortions, rumination
- **Behavioral imbalance**: Unhealthy habits, compulsive patterns, avoidance

## Healing Approaches

### Awareness-Based Healing
Using Right Awakening techniques to directly observe and understand the root causes of imbalance.

### Cognitive Restructuring
Identifying and transforming limiting belief patterns.

### Emotional Regulation
Developing skills for recognizing, accepting, and skillfully processing emotions.

## Practice

Daily awareness practice is the foundation of life healing. By consistently observing internal states without judgment, you create the conditions for natural healing and integration.

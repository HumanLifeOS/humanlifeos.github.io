# Life Mechanism

Understanding how the human life system operates at the mechanism level.

## Stimulus-Response Chain

```
External Stimulus
    ↓
Sensory Perception
    ↓
Cognitive Interpretation
    ↓
Emotional Response
    ↓
Behavioral Pattern
    ↓
Life Result
```

## Key Mechanisms

### Attention Allocation
How attention is distributed across sensory inputs, thoughts, and emotions.

### Emotional Processing
How emotions are generated, regulated, and transformed.

### Cognitive Patterns
How mental habits, biases, and schemas develop and influence behavior.

## Upgrading Mechanisms

Understanding the default instinct-driven mechanisms is the first step toward upgrading them to awareness-driven mechanisms.

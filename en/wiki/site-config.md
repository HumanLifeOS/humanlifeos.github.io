# Life Training

Systematic training programs for upgrading the human life operating system.

## Training Levels

### Level 1: Foundation
- Breath awareness
- Body scan
- Basic emotion recognition

### Level 2: Awareness
- Advanced perception training
- Cognitive insight development
- Emotional pattern recognition

### Level 3: Understanding
- Deep mental pattern analysis
- Belief system examination
- Self-model deconstruction

### Level 4: Wisdom
- Ego transcendence
- Unity experience
- Life integration

## Training Formats

- **Daily practice**: 15-30 minutes per day
- **Weekend workshops**: Intensive group training
- **Residential camps**: Deep immersion programs

# Life Structure

Understanding the structural components of the human life system.

## Life Hardware

The physical body, including:
- Brain and nervous system
- Physiological systems (cardiovascular, digestive, endocrine, etc.)
- Sensory organs

## Life Software

Mental and emotional patterns, including:
- Cognitive schemas
- Emotional response patterns
- Behavioral habits
- Belief systems

## Life Operating System

The awareness and consciousness layer that manages the interaction between hardware and software.

## Summary

The life system consists of three layers: hardware (body), software (mind), and the operating system (awareness). Upgrade means moving from instinct-driven operation to awareness-driven operation.
